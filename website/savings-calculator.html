How we work these numbers out

  • Supply consolidation — 15%: your annual consumables spend × 15%. Homes buying from many suppliers usually pay more per item and lose volume discounts.
  • Waste reduction — half: your annual spend × your waste percentage × 50%. Better par levels and stock rotation typically remove around half of avoidable waste.
  • Admin time — 30%: your weekly admin hours × 52 × 30%, valued at £15 per hour. Fewer suppliers and simpler ordering means far less chasing.
  • Total: the two cash savings plus the money value of the time given back.

These are estimates based on typical results across UK care homes, not a guarantee. Your free operational review gives you figures based on your actual invoices and stock. All figures in pounds sterling (£).
