Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17/08/2026

Sennicare (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We handle personal data in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Sennicare, [YOUR REGISTERED ADDRESS]. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email hello@sennicare.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and care home name, when you send us an enquiry.
  • Any details you choose to include in your message to us.
  • Operational information you share with us during a review (for example supplier and spend data), which we treat as confidential business information.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange your free operational review.
  • To carry out any work you engage us to do.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your data, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your clear consent.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Records relating to client work are kept for [6] years, as required for tax and accounting purposes.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers we need in order to operate — for example our email provider and our accountant — and only where they are bound to keep it secure. We will never pass your data to suppliers or product vendors.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. If we later add website analytics, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
