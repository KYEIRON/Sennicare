The way it works today

Four ways a care home quietly loses money every month

None of them are anyone’s fault. They are what happens when ordering is spread across a dozen suppliers and nobody has time to check.

Twelve suppliers, twelve invoices

A separate login, a separate delivery and a separate invoice to check for every category. Volume gets split, so no one gives you a volume price.

Typically 15–25% above a consolidated price

Nobody knows if the price is fair

Without a benchmark you are taking a rep’s word for it. Prices drift upward between contract reviews and nothing flags it.

Invisible until someone checks

Two days a week on ordering

Placing orders, chasing deliveries, checking invoices, counting stock. Senior time that should be on the floor with residents.

About £7,800 a year at 10 hours a week

The cheapest option can be unsafe

Powdered latex gloves. A pad with no wetness indicator. A perfumed wash on fragile skin. Buying on price alone reaches residents.

A cost that never shows on the invoice

Questions

The things managers ask us first

Do suppliers pay you to be recommended?

No. We take no commission, no listing fee and no referral payment from any supplier, ever. You pay us, which is exactly why our ranking can be trusted. If that ever changed, this page would say so.

What happens to our existing suppliers and contracts?

Nothing, unless you decide otherwise. Sennicare shows you what else is available at what price — it does not cancel anything or contact anyone on your behalf. Plenty of homes use it to check that their current supplier is still competitive, and stay put.

We already use a buying group. Is this the same thing?

No. A buying group negotiates on your behalf and usually earns a rebate from the suppliers in it, so its list is the list you get. Sennicare has no financial relationship with any supplier and shows you everything, including options your buying group doesn’t carry.

Is our data safe?

Your care home details, searches and orders sit in your own account and are visible only to you. No other subscriber can see them. Nothing is shared with a supplier until you choose to send a request — and when you do, it goes from your own email, so their reply comes straight back to you. You can download or delete everything we hold at any time.

How do you decide what is “suitable for elderly care”?

Every product carries a rating out of five and a plain yes or no on suitability, based on the things that matter in a care setting: latex and fragrance in skin-contact products, absorbency and wetness indicators in continence products, dignity in dining products, and the relevant standards such as EN 455 or ISO 13485. Anything rated below our floor is shown but never recommended.

Will this help at our next CQC inspection?

It gives you a written record of what you ordered, from whom, at what price and against what standards — which is the sort of evidence inspectors ask for when they look at how a home is run. We are not affiliated with the CQC and cannot speak for them.

What does “free” actually mean?

You can create an account, set up your home and run searches without giving us a card. Paid plans lift the monthly search limit and add history, exports and multiple homes. There is no contract and no notice period — cancel from inside your account.
