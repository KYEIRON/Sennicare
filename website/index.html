About Us

Operations people, on your side of the table

Sennicare is an independent consultancy working exclusively with UK care homes. We are not a supplier, a software vendor or a buying group — we are paid by you, which means every recommendation we make is in your interest and yours alone.

Our work is deliberately practical. We look at what you buy and who you buy it from, at what sits in your stock cupboards, and at the admin your managers and nurses do every week. Then we remove the cost and the wasted hours, and we stay involved to make sure the improvements hold.

Every engagement starts the same way: a free, no-obligation operational review of your home.
